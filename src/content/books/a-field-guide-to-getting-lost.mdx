## Why this book matters

There is something useful about being reminded that uncertainty is not always a failure.

Sometimes getting lost is the beginning of noticing.

## Notes

The book creates space for wandering, both physically and inwardly.

It makes a strong case for leaving some parts of life unresolved.

## Lessons

- Uncertainty can create space for discovery.
- Travel changes the scale of familiar problems.
- Not every direction needs to be decided immediately.
