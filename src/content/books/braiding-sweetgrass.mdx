## First impression

This book asks the reader to pay closer attention to the living world.

It is generous, patient, and deeply aware of the relationship between knowledge and responsibility.

## Notes

The idea of reciprocity stays with me.

To receive something from the world is also to become responsible for how we respond to it.

## Lessons

- Attention is an ethical act.
- Growth is rarely an individual process.
- The natural world is full of instruction.
