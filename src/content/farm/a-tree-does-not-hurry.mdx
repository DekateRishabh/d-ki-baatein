## Growth is quiet at first

A tree does not announce its growth.

For a long time, it appears to be standing still. The branches remain almost the same. The shade is barely different. The distance between one season and the next is difficult to measure.

Then, one morning, the change becomes impossible to miss.

A new branch reaches further than before. The leaves make a different shape against the sky. The space beneath the tree becomes cooler.

## Learning to notice slowly

The Farm has made me more patient with invisible progress.

Some changes need time before they can be seen. Roots grow before branches. Soil improves before anything appears above it. A place becomes more alive long before it looks finished.

This is difficult to remember when working quickly.

## What the tree teaches

The tree does not hurry because it does not need to prove that it is growing.

Its work continues whether or not anyone is watching.

That feels like a useful thing to remember.
