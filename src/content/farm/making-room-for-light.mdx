## A longer view

A small change to the landscape opened a longer view toward the trees.

Nothing major was removed. A few things were moved, and a little more space was allowed between them.

The result was not dramatic, but it changed the feeling of the place.

## Space is also part of design

A landscape is shaped by what is planted, but also by what is left open.

Empty space allows light to travel. It gives trees room to be seen. It creates a pause between one part of the Farm and another.

The most useful changes are sometimes the quietest ones.

## Continuing to observe

The space will change again as the trees grow.

That is part of the difficulty and pleasure of working with living things. Every decision is temporary because the place continues to respond.
