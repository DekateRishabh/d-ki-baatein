## After the first rain

The garden changed colour after the first sustained rain.

The soil became darker. Leaves that had looked tired for weeks seemed to recover overnight. Small sounds returned: insects, birds, water moving through the edges of the land.

Rain makes a place more visible.

## The garden as a record

Every season leaves a different kind of mark.

Summer shows what can survive heat. The monsoon reveals where water gathers. Winter makes the structure of the landscape clearer.

The garden is not one fixed thing. It is a record of weather, care, neglect, and return.

## A beginning, not a result

The first rain did not finish anything.

It simply made the next stage possible.

That seems to be how most things grow: not through one dramatic moment, but through a series of small conditions becoming favourable.
