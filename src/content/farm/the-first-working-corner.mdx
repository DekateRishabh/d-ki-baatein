## A useful corner

A simple corner began to take shape near the garden.

The intention was not to build a perfect workspace. It was to make a place where work could happen without becoming disconnected from the land around it.

A table, some shade, enough light, and a view of something growing.

## Making a place usable

A place becomes meaningful through use.

The first day of work changed the corner more than the construction itself. A notebook was left on the table. A cup stayed beside it. The space began to collect evidence of ordinary life.

## Still unfinished

The corner is not complete.

There are still changes to make, and some decisions will only become clear after living with the space for a while.

That is acceptable.

The Farm is not being built as a finished image. It is being built as a place to return to.
