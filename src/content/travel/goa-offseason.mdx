## The coast without the noise

Goa in the off-season feels like a different place.

The beaches are quieter. The roads have more space. The afternoon seems to stretch without asking to be filled.

There is a particular freedom in visiting a familiar place when it is not performing for visitors.

The coast becomes less of an attraction and more of a landscape again.

## Slow afternoons

Most days began without a fixed plan.

A walk near the water. A long lunch. A few pages of a book. The sound of waves continuing without any concern for the hour.

Nothing dramatic happened.

That was part of the reason to stay.

Some journeys are valuable because they give us new experiences. Others are valuable because they return us to a slower version of ourselves.

## The shape of quiet

By evening, the light became softer.

The beach emptied gradually. Footprints disappeared beneath the next wave. The horizon remained almost unchanged.

I began to understand that quiet is not the absence of life.

It is a different way of paying attention to it.
